Research Spreadsheet v0.4.7 Stable

UI Stability final release.

Kept from the validated v0.4.6 architecture:
- RibbonGroup is created with RibbonPage as its parent.
- Ribbon groups remain ordinary non-native child widgets.
- Responsive overflow does not reparent ribbon groups.
- Resize-driven overflow updates remain debounced.
- v0.4.1 startup model guard and v0.4 reliability improvements are retained.

Removed from the diagnostic build:
- application-wide QWidget event filter
- qInstallMessageHandler geometry interception
- ribbon_diagnostics.log generation
- startup top-level widget snapshots
- RibbonGroup lifecycle/resize diagnostic logging

This is the normal-operation UI Stability build.
